Official School Notice • 12 November 2026

End-of-Year Closing Letter, Important Administrative Dates & 2027 School Fees

To: All Parents, Guardians, and Learners • Windsor Secondary School

Dear Parents, Guardians, and Learners,
As our learners complete their final examination papers, we officially bring the 2026 academic year to a close. This milestone marks the culmination of months of hard work, perseverance, and shared dedication across our entire school community. On behalf of the School Governing Body (SGB), the School Management Team (SMT), and the dedicated educators and support staff of Windsor Secondary School, I would like to express my deepest gratitude to every parent and guardian for your continued trust, cooperation, and active involvement in your child's education.

Throughout 2026, your encouragement at home, attendance at school meetings, and commitment to upholding our school values have played a vital role in maintaining a focused and disciplined learning environment.

To our dear learners: we commend you for the resilience, focus, and determination you have demonstrated throughout the year—both in the classroom and during the final examination period. As you enter the festive break, we urge you to rest well, make wise choices, and continue to uphold the values of excellence and integrity. Always remember our guiding words: “Success is not the end, but the beginning of greater things.”

Official Notice: School Fees Increase for the 2027 Academic Year

Following the Annual General Meeting held on 31 October 2026 and as formally resolved with the School Governing Body (SGB), parents and guardians are hereby notified that school fees for the 2027 academic year will increase to R3,000.00 per learner for the full year. This adjustment enables the school to maintain quality teaching resources, facility upkeep, and learner support programmes.

Parents with queries or wanting to make early payment arrangements are invited to the School Fees session on 20 November 2026 (08:00 – 12:00).

Mr. K. R. Mbatha Principal • Windsor Secondary School
